Why 127 IQR-flagged data points were intentionally preserved for model integrity.
Methodology Highlight

Applying the standard Interquartile Range (IQR) rule across the entire dataset flags 127 weeks (1.97% of observations) as high-sales outliers.

Key Diagnostic Finding:

100% of these 127 outliers belong to large flagship stores (Stores 2, 4, 13, 14, 20) during Thanksgiving (Black Friday) and the Christmas rush. None represent data entry errors or anomalous sensor malfunctions.

Evaluation Defense Rationale: In commercial retail supply chain planning, deleting high holiday weeks creates artificial stock-outs during peak revenue windows. True seasonal peaks must be retained and modeled via calendar/lag features.

Simpson's Paradox & Pooled vs Within-Store
Core Econometric Takeaway for Lab Viva:

External macro indicators (CPI, Fuel Price, Unemployment, Temperature) explain less than 1% of weekly sales variation once Store Identity (capacity/format) and Annual Seasonality (lag_52) are included. While Fuel Price and CPI rose steadily between 2010 and 2012, grocery and retail demand remained anchored by fixed store scale and holiday traditions.

The "Christmas Week" Anomaly

Evaluation Highlight

In the Walmart dataset, the official Holiday_Flag = 1 for Christmas is placed on the week of Dec 31, 2010 and Dec 30, 2011 (post-Christmas / New Year eve).

The True Rush is the Pre-Christmas Week (Dec 24 / Dec 23):

The week preceding Christmas (unflagged, Holiday_Flag = 0) registered average chain sales of over $1.79 Million per store (chain total > $80 Million), even higher than the flagged week!

Recommendation: A production model must incorporate a dedicated "Pre-Christmas Week" indicator feature rather than relying solely on official calendar flags.

Skill Lab Evaluation Rubric
Slide 01 • Problem Definition & Objectives

Walmart Store Sales Forecasting & Econometric Analysis

Business Problem: Retail hypermarkets must anticipate fluctuating customer demand across multi-store chains to optimize inventory replenishment, warehouse staffing, and supply-chain logistics.

  • Dataset Universe: 45 Walmart retail supercenters across the United States.
  • Temporal Span: 143 consecutive weeks from February 5, 2010 to October 26, 2012 (6,435 observations; 0 missing values).
  • Core Objective: Predict weekly sales per store with minimal error and determine whether external macroeconomic indicators (CPI, fuel, unemployment) drive sales or if internal historical seasonality dominates.
Slide 02 • Data Acquisition & Cleaning Protocol

Data Quality Verification & Outlier Treatment

  • Completeness: Verified regular 7-day intervals for all 45 stores across the entire 143-week duration. Original raw data was kept strictly intact.
  • Date Standardization: Resolved day-first formatting (05-02-2010 = 5th February 2010) and derived temporal coordinates (Year, Month, WeekOfYear).
  • Outlier Decision ("Investigate, Do Not Delete"): The IQR formula flags 127 weeks (2.0%). All 127 belong to large stores during Thanksgiving and Christmas. In commercial forecasting, deleting real holiday revenue peaks causes severe under-stocking.
Slide 03 • Exploratory Data Analysis & Hypotheses

Key Empirical Insights & Econometric Realities

  • Store Scale Disparity: Stores differ by up to 7.8× in volume (Store #2 averages $2.66M/wk vs Store #43 at $0.34M/wk), reflecting different supercenter formats and urban footfall.
  • Holiday Surge: Holiday weeks generate a +30.2% average chain-wide volume boost. Thanksgiving generates a 1.43× multiplier, and the pre-Christmas rush generates 1.65×.
  • The Macro Myth: External economic factors (CPI, Fuel Price, Unemployment) have near-zero correlation with weekly store variations. Consumer retail grocery shopping is non-discretionary.
Slide 04 • Feature Engineering Architecture

Time-Series Lags & Momentum Formulation

Because weekly retail sales are strongly autocorrelated, we engineered domain-specific temporal lags:

  • lag_1: Immediate short-term inertia (sales of the prior week).
  • lag_52: Annual seasonal anchor (sales of the exact same week in the prior calendar year).
  • roll_mean_4: 4-week moving average capturing recent quarter momentum.
  • Train/Test Integrity: Split chronologically (80% Train / 20% Test) on June 22, 2012. Testing is strictly evaluated on 855 unseen future store-weeks.

Slide 06 • Critical Limitations & Next Steps

Honest Academic Reflection & Future Enhancements

  • Temporal Horizon Constraint: The test set (Jun–Oct 2012) only contained one holiday (Labor Day) and ended before Thanksgiving/Christmas peaks.
  • Lag Dependency: Requiring lag_52 consumes the first full year of data, reducing the modeling dataset to 91 weeks per store.
  • Uncaptured Variables: Department-level breakdowns, competitor proximity, and circular markdown promotions were unavailable in the top-level store dataset.
  • Production Roadmap: Implement LightGBM with rolling multi-step walk-forward cross-validation and explicit promotional discount flags.
